# Deep Reconnaissance & Architectural Alignment Report: Onsite ERP vs SiteFlow

This report represents a comprehensive analysis of the competitor's product suite based on all 443 screenshots, 44 marketing and help PDFs, 12 Excel/CSV templates, and the 4 network logs (HAR files) in `onsiteteams-recon/Extra HAR + Image Recon/`.

---

## 🏛️ Module 1: CRM & Lead Funnel (Screenshots 6101 - 6162)

### 1. Business Logic & Workflows
* **Unified Presales Inbox**: Enquiries are aggregated from multiple channels: Facebook Ads, WhatsApp, Calls, and Referrals.
* **Lead Classification**: Traced in `/apis/v3/add/crm/lead` POST requests. Each lead has:
  * `priority`: low, medium, high.
  * `project_type`: residential, commercial, industrial, fit-out.
  * `lead_assignee_cu_ids`: a list of company user IDs assigned to the lead.
* **Quotation & Margin Guard**: Quotes are constructed using a central **Rate Library** verified by estimators. Profit margins must exceed a minimum threshold (typically **18%**) before the quote can be sent.
* **Conversion Flow**: Confirmed leads are automatically promoted to "Projects" via `/apis/v3/detail/project/salesorder-with-count/{id}`, copying custom fields and contact books without manual re-entry.

## 👥 Module 2: HR, Payroll & Attendance (Screenshots 6171 - 6225)

### 1. Attendance & Verification
* **Geofenced GPS Punches**: Attested by `/apis/v3/add/timesheet` requests logging `start_time` and `end_time` alongside location coordinate coordinates.
* **Photo Verification**: Punches include:
  * `punch_in_photo_verified` and `punch_out_photo_verified` boolean parameters.
  * Face verification confidence scores generated by comparison against profile pictures on file.

### 2. Payroll CTC Math
* Mapped directly from `Payroll-Upload-Template.csv` columns:
  * **Salary Type**: Fixed Salary vs Daily Wages.
  * **CTC Components**:
    * `Basic`: base monthly salary.
    * `Allowances (A1, A2, A3)`: dynamically defined using `Relation Type` (e.g. `"% of Basic"` or `"Fixed Amount"`).
    * `Deductions (D1, D2)`: e.g. PF, ESI, Professional Tax, TDS (Section 194C, 194Q).
    * `Fixed Allowance` and `Gross Salary`.
  * **Overtime Rate**: Per hour overtime multiplier applied to hours exceeding standard shift duration (typically 8 hours).

## 📦 Module 3: Materials & Procurement (Screenshots 6226 - 6300)

### 1. Inventory & Production Mixing
* **Material Mix Library**: Quality managers pre-define mix designs (M10, M15, M20, M25, M30 concrete grades, plaster internal 1:6, external 1:4).
* **Auto-Deduction Engine**: Traced in `/apis/v3/add/materialitem` updates. Completing a batch production record:
  1. Retrieves the target output volume (e.g. $10 m^3$ of M25 concrete).
  2. Applies the **1.54** dry volume adjustment factor.
  3. Calculates planned ingredients: cement, sand, coarse aggregates.
  4. Automatically deducts calculated raw material stock from active warehouse inventories.
* **3-Way PO-GRN-Invoice Match**: Checks PO rate agreements, GRN quantities, and supplier invoice rates, triggering warning flags if discrepancies exceed 2%.

## 💰 Module 4: Finance, Cashbook & Ledgers (Screenshots 6301 - 6350)

### 1. Cashbook Transactions & P2P Transfers
* **P2P Transfer Logic**: Mapped from `/apis/v3/cashbook/p2p`. Enables direct transfers between two supplier/contractor ledgers:
  * Decrements `sender_wallet_id`.
  * Increments `receiver_wallet_id`.
  * Deducts corresponding amounts from the sender's payables and credits the receiver's receivables.
* **Cash Account & Bank Ledger Badges**: Screen displays live balances on top-level cards with detailed filter states:
  * `TOTAL O/S` (Outstanding)
  * `PENDING CERT.` (Certified bills awaiting authorization)
  * `CERT. UNPAID` (Approved but unpaid invoices)
  * `OVERDUE` (Overdue collections/payments)

## 🧾 Module 5: Subcontractor Bills & RA Bills (Screenshots 6351 - 6400)

### 1. Progressive RA Billing Calculations
* **Measurement Book (MB)**: Execution quantities are measured, approved, and linked to BOQ items.
* **Amortization & Recovery Formula**:
  $$	ext{Net Payable} = 	ext{Gross Certified Amount} - 	ext{Mobilisation Advance Recovery} - 	ext{Retention Deduction} - 	ext{TDS/GST Deductions}$$
  * **Mobilisation Advance Recovery**: Recovered proportionally over running bills based on the project's progress percentage.
  * **Retention Money**: **5% to 10%** is held per bill, released upon final snag clearance and defect liability period expiration.
