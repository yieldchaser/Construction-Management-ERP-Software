FAQ
What is construction project management software?
Construction project management software helps contractors and developers plan, track, and control every stage of construction
projects. Onsite’s platform offers BOQ-linked planning, DPR automation, subcontractor billing, and real-time progress tracking,
making it a complete solution for owners and site teams.
How does Onsite construction project management software help in construction progress tracking?
Onsite acts as a construction progress tracking software by capturing Daily Progress Reports (DPRs) with photos, videos, and quantity
updates. It provides real-time dashboards so project managers and owners can monitor site activities from anywhere.
Why do construction companies need digital project management software?
Manual tracking through spreadsheets and WhatsApp leads to delays, miscommunication, and cost overruns. A digital project
management platform like Onsite construction project management software ensures real-time updates, budget control, and better
coordination between site and office teams.
Does Onsite construction project management software support BOQ-based project planning?
Yes. Onsite construction project management software links every material request and task schedule to the Bill of Quantities (BOQ),
helping businesses maintain budget vs actual cost control throughout the project.
