FAQs
Is this accounting software or project finance software?
Onsite is construction-specific financial management software. It includes accounting capabilities but goes beyond traditional
accounting by integrating project budgets, BOQs, procurement, progress tracking, and billing into one system. Think of it as
accounting software that understands construction workflows.
Can I track profitability per project, per tower, per floor?
Yes. Onsite supports multi-level cost allocation. You can track finances at the company level, project level, tower/building level, floor
level, and even activity level. This gives you granular visibility into where money is being spent and which sections are profitable.
Can my CA/accountant use it easily?
Yes. Onsite is designed for both construction teams and finance professionals. Your accountant can access project-wise ledgers, trial
balances, GST reports, and financial statements without needing to learn construction terminology. We also support Tally and
QuickBooks integration for final book closing.
Can I control approvals for expenses and bills?
Yes. Onsite supports multi-level approval workflows. You can define who needs to approve purchase orders, vendor bills, labour
payments, and budget revisions based on amount thresholds and project hierarchies. Nothing gets processed without proper
authorization.
Can Onsite integrate with my existing accounting software?
Yes, Onsite construction financial management software integrates with popular accounting platforms like QuickBooks and Tally. You
can also use Onsite as a standalone construction accounting software with complete financial management capabilities
