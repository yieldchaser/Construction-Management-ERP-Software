19 June 2026 NEW FEATURE
Task Planning: Actuals, Forecasts, Delay Detection & Replan
Onsite now tracks planned and actual dates separately. When a task starts, the system captures the
actual start date. When it completes, the actual end date is recorded. Your original plan stays intact
for comparison. No more overwriting history.
WHAT’S NEW
✓ Forecasted End Date , based on current progress pace, Onsite calculates when a task is likely to finish
and updates the forecast automatically with every progress entry.
✓ Delay Detection , every task is automatically flagged as On Track, At Risk, Delayed, or Early. Delayed and
At Risk tasks prompt the site team to log a reason and category (weather, material shortage, labour,
subcontractor, etc.).
✓ Status Change History , every status change is logged with who changed it, when, and what they said.
Full audit trail, no manual tracking needed.
✓ One-Click Replan , when actuals diverge from the plan, the Replan button saves the current schedule as
a baseline, pushes actual dates in, and recalculates all downstream tasks based on dependencies.
✓ Holidays & Weekoffs , task durations and forecasts now account for your company’s holidays and
weekly off days, with per-project overrides for sites on different schedules.

Bank Account Management: Fund Transfers & Account Statement
Manage your bank accounts directly in Onsite. Record fund movements between accounts, track cash

submit for approval. Cash balance and bank balance update together automatically.
