FAQs
What is Onsite’s Construction Reporting and Analytics Software?
Onsite’s construction reporting and analytics software is a centralized system that converts live site data into structured reports,
dashboards, and performance insights. It helps construction companies track project progress, costs, productivity, and overall project
health in real time—without relying on manual Excel-based reporting.
Why do construction companies need reporting and analytics software?
Construction projects generate large volumes of data across sites, teams, and processes. Without a reporting and analytics system,
this data remains fragmented and delayed. Onsite automates progress reporting, cost analysis, and KPI tracking—allowing teams to
identify issues early, reduce decision delays, and maintain better control over execution.
Does Onsite provide real-time project dashboards?
Yes. Onsite offers real-time project dashboards that visually display key metrics such as progress percentage, cost performance,
productivity trends, and pending actions. These dashboards update automatically as site data is recorded, giving management live
visibility without waiting for manual updates.
How does Onsite help with cost vs budget analytics?
Onsite continuously compares planned budgets against actual expenses recorded from procurement, labour, and site usage. This
allows teams to track deviations early, identify cost leakages, and take corrective actions before overruns escalate.
How does Onsite ensure my reports are always accurate?
Onsite syncs data in real time from your site teams, procurement entries, bills, and progress updates — ensuring your reports always
reflect the latest status.
Can I customize reports based on my business needs?
Yes. You can filter by project, vendor, activity, date range, department, and even user-defined fields.
